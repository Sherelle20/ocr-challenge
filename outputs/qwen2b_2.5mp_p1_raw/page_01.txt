1. Aggressive breast cancer linked to obesity-driven diabetes

More than 120 million Americans suffer from diabetes or pre-diabetes. Triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) is the most aggressive form of breast cancer, and TNBC patients with obesity-driven diabetes often have worse outcomes.

A new study by researchers from Boston University Chobanian and Amdiesian School of Medicine helps explain why this happens and suggests a potential way to improve treatment for these patients.

At the present, oncologists do not consider patients with breast cancer and obesity-driven diabetes different in any significant way from patients with breast cancer who are other wise healthy.
