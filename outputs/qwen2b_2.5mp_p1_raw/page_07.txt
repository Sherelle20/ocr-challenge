Other tribe of Kenya have adapted more readily to the "progress" of modern times. In contrast, the Maasai have resisted in their tradition ways, so as Kenya takes more land for growing tribes and agriculture, they suffer.

Lions are disappearing. Species: their number plunged from 100,000 ten years ago, to about 41,000 today. Maasai Wilderness Conservation Trust has pioneered a compensation program, reimbursing livestock killed by lion and other predators. The Program is supported by camp ya Kanzi tourist conservation fees and employs warriors as lion scouts. In the past, the Maasai and the wildlife simply lived together, in balance. If this could be re-established by showing to the Maasai the economic value of the presence of wildlife in their land, the future of the land of the wildlife and of the Maasai people will be assured.
